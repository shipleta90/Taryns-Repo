Privacy Policy

Personal Agent

Last updated October 15, 2026

Who this app is for

Personal Agent is a private assistant built for and used only by Taryn Cardona. It is not offered to the public, and no one else's account can be connected to it.

What Google data it uses, and why

With the account owner's permission, the app accesses:

  • Gmail: to read recent messages, sort out unwanted promotional email (moved to Trash, never permanently deleted), summarize newsletters, and send or save emails that the owner has reviewed and approved.
  • Google Calendar: to see existing events and create events that the owner has reviewed and approved.

The app never sends an email or creates an event without the owner's explicit approval in the app.

Where the data is stored

The app runs on the owner's own computer. Email, calendar and settings data are stored only on that computer, and sign-in credentials are kept in its secure system keychain. The app is reachable only over the owner's private network, not the public internet.

Who it is shared with

Data is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone for their own purposes. To carry out tasks the owner asks for (for example, drafting a reply), the relevant message text may be processed by Anthropic's Claude API, which acts as a service provider. Content that looks like health, legal or financial information, or that contains identifiers such as Social Security or card numbers, is filtered out first except where the owner has asked the assistant to handle that specific conversation.

Limited use

Personal Agent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features described above, is not used to train general-purpose AI models, and is not read by any person other than the owner.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's data folder on the owner's computer removes everything it stored.
