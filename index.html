About

Ten years building the operating systems behind hypergrowth teams at Amazon and Allergan — now for companies earlier in that climb.

Most recently, I led technical PMO, operations, and strategy for Allē, Allergan’s tech innovation hub — scaling product and engineering 200% year‑over‑year, securing $196M in infrastructure funding, and building the planning cadence behind $500M+ in revenue. Before that, at Amazon and AWS, I built programs supporting 50,000+ employees across 56 countries and scaled teams 108% in four months. I’m also a licensed attorney (J.D., Washington University School of Law) — it shows up in how I build systems: durable, cross‑functional, and built to hold up under real pressure. Now I bring that same discipline to smaller, growing companies — embedded, part‑time, not a report handed off at the end of a project.
