About

The systems that get a company to its first ten hires rarely survive the next fifty.

I work with founders and executive teams who’ve outgrown ad hoc — where decisions, planning, and execution still run through one person’s memory instead of a repeatable operating structure. My work is building that structure: the operating cadence, planning frameworks, and cross‑functional discipline that let a company keep moving fast without everything depending on improvisation. It’s embedded, part‑time work — not a report handed off at the end of a project.
